Build a GOAT

Privacy Policy · Effective November 13, 2026

Build a GOAT ("the app") is an independent single-player game developed by Urmat Okonov. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not make network requests.

Data on your device

Game progress, such as the fighters you build, may be saved locally on your device so you can continue playing. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
